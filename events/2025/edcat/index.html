EdCAT 2025: Education, Careers and Technology Summit

The ACS Education, Careers and Technology Summit in Hobart, with a dedicated career stream attended by UTAS ICT students.

Wrest Point, Sandy Bay, Hobart

Thursday 10 April 2025 · 8:15 AM – 6:00 PM

Summit CareerNetworkingIndustry Open to students, educators and tech professionals
Outcome: Career advice from recruiters and early-career professionals

About this event

EdCAT, the Education, Careers and Technology Summit, is a full-day event run by the Australian Computer Society (ACS) at Wrest Point in Hobart. The 2025 summit offered several streams covering careers, technology leadership and education, plus an expo with industry partners.

UTAS ICT students attended to meet industry professionals and hear talks ranging from AI development to job hunting strategies. The career stream included a panel of early-career professionals from ACS, Hydro Tasmania, the Department of Premier and Cabinet and the Department for Education.
