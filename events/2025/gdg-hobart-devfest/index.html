GDG Hobart DevFest 2025: AI in Action and Career Insights

GDG Hobart's DevFest at UTAS, with a Google engineer on AI in practice and UTAS alumni on moving from university into tech.

Centenary Building, UTAS Sandy Bay

Wednesday 12 November 2025 · 5:15 PM – 6:45 PM

Meetup CareerNetworking Open to students, job seekers and professionals
Outcome: 25+ attendees · alumni career Q&A

About this event

DevFest Hobart 2025 brought developers, students and tech enthusiasts together at the University of Tasmania, which provided the venue. Oscar Wahltinez, Senior Software Engineer at Google, flew in from Sydney to present practical examples of AI and machine learning, including an interactive AI game.

UTAS alumni Amber Nguyen (Hydro Tasmania) and Pul Khut (Department of Police, Fire and Emergency Management) shared how they moved from university into the tech industry and how AI is shaping career paths, followed by a Q&A session.
