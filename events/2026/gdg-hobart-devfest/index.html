GDG Hobart DevFest 2026: AI-Powered Development & Digital Analytics

GDG Hobart's second DevFest session of 2026 at UTAS: a hands-on workshop on Gemini in Android Studio with Kartik Arora (Bilue), then a Looker Studio dashboards masterclass with Cynthia Wilanda (MyState).

Room 132, Centenary Building, UTAS Sandy Bay

Thursday 19 November 2026 · 5:15 PM – 6:45 PM

1 / 4

About this event

GDG Hobart held DevFest 2026: Explore AI-Powered Development & Digital Analytics on Thursday 19 November 2026, from 5:15 PM to 6:45 PM, in Room 132 of the Centenary Building on the University of Tasmania's Sandy Bay campus. It was GDG Hobart's second DevFest session of the year, and with a 62-seat room, RSVPs were capped at 60.

In the first part, Kartik Arora, Lead Engineer at Bilue, ran a hands-on workshop on building Android apps faster with Gemini in Android Studio. Working on a real multi-module project, attendees used Agent Mode to scaffold new features, turned wireframes into Jetpack Compose screens, and tried the Android CLI and automated testing.

In the second part, Cynthia Wilanda, Digital Analytics and Conversion Lead at MyState Limited, showed how to bring data from Google Analytics, Google Play Console and other Google tools into Looker Studio, and turn scattered metrics into clear dashboards for product decisions.

The evening finished with refreshments and time to talk with the speakers, other students and local developers. GDG Hobart thanked UTAS for the venue and its volunteers for running check-in and helping during the workshop.

For UTAS ICT students, it was a practical look at how AI tools are used in real mobile development, and at the analytics skills that product and data roles ask for.
