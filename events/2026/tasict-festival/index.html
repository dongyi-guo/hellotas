About this event

Tas Tech Fest, run by TasICT, brings together industry leaders, innovators, government and the broader ICT community for a full day of technology insights, collaboration and connection. The 2026 event was held on Wednesday 18 March at Wrest Point in Hobart, after the 2025 event booked out.

The program featured a lineup of industry speakers and two exhibitions. Student pricing and eligibility are still to be confirmed.
