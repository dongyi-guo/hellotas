GDG Hobart: Build with AI & Break Into Security

A two-part GDG Hobart evening at UTAS on building analyst agents with BigQuery and breaking into a cyber security career.

Room 132, Centenary Building, UTAS Sandy Bay

Thursday 5 March 2026 · 5:15 PM – 6:45 PM

Meetup CareerNetworking FREE Open to students, early-career professionals and anyone curious about cyber security
Outcome: 45+ attendees · Google security engineer Q&A

About this event

GDG Hobart opened its 2026 program with a free two-part community session at the University of Tasmania's Centenary Building. Thiébaud W., Senior Security Engineer at Google, explained the different areas of cyber security, the skills valued by industry and realistic entry points into security roles, followed by a live Q&A.

The Build with AI session covered building analyst agents with BigQuery, with speakers Thiébaud W. and Yash Mehta thanked for sharing their expertise. More than 45 people attended, almost filling the room, and Professor Nicole Herbert, Head of the School of ICT, described it as a brilliant turnout.
