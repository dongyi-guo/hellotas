ICT Awards Night 2026

On Monday evening, the University of Tasmania’s School of ICT celebrated the achievements of its students and graduates, with over 150 people joining across Hobart and Launceston. Around 30 awards and around 100 certificates of unit excellence were presented.

University of Tasmania School of ICT · Hobart and Launceston

Monday 9 November 2026 · Inaugural event

1 / 14

About this event

The University of Tasmania (UTAS) School of ICT held its inaugural ICT Awards Night on Monday 9 November 2026, with more than 150 students, graduates, industry partners and staff joining across Hobart and Launceston. The idea for the evening came from students, who were also invited to help shape the awards by telling the School which achievements and contributions deserved recognition. Every award presented on the night was sponsored by industry partners.

Around 30 awards and around 100 Unit Excellence certificates were presented. The certificates recognised students who achieved the highest academic result in their unit in 2026, in units ranging from first-year undergraduate subjects to final-year Master’s units. The awards also celebrated students who create amazing projects, engage with industry, demonstrate leadership, volunteer, support their classmates and make the School a better place to study and belong, including 27 nominations for the Outstanding ICT Project Awards.

The four highest honours recognised research excellence, academic excellence in the Bachelor of Information and Communication Technology and the Master of Information Technology and Systems, and the 2026 ICT Graduate of the Year, awarded to Gazi MD Wasi-UL Hoque Eusha. The Students’ Choice Awards, shaped by students themselves, included the Inquisitor Award for the student who asks the questions everyone else is secretly hoping someone will ask, and the ICT Community Spirit Award went to Chhunly Chhek. Two students, Rakhitha Dassanayake in Launceston and Taylah Stewart in Hobart, also shared student addresses during the evening.
