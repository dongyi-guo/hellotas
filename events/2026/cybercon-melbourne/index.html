AISA CyberCon 2026 Melbourne

Five UTAS students represented the University at the Australian Cyber Conference with School of ICT sponsorship.

Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre, Melbourne

14–16 October 2026 · Three days

Conference CareerTravelCyber Security Five selected students
Outcome: Five sponsored places · three days of cyber security learning

About this event

Five University of Tasmania students were selected to represent UTAS at CyberCon 2026, the Australian Cyber Conference run by the Australian Information Security Association (AISA) at the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre. Their attendance was sponsored by the School of ICT, with thanks to Dr David Herbert and Professor Nicole Herbert.

Day 1 opened with a keynote from Jen Easterly, CEO of RSAC and former Director of the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA). Students also attended sessions on cyber security education, building a cyber range on a student budget, and the CTRL+ALT+COMPETE live cyber security game show.
